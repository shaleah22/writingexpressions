Writing Expressions

+ Add

  • plus, sum
  • more than
  • increased by
  • added to

− Subtract

  • minus, difference
  • decreased by, take away
  • less than
  • fewer than
  • subtract … from

× Multiply

  • times, product
  • multiplied by
  • groups of
  • twice, triple

÷ Divide

  • divided by, quotient
  • split equally
  • half of

Highlighted = flip the order.
5 less than n → n − 5

Number before letter: 3n
